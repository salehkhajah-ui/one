WELCOME, CAPTAIN

How to run your port
1
Dock the ships Ships arrive and wait offshore — a red ⚓ Ship waiting button appears at the top. Click it (or the ship itself), then press DOCK SHIP. The beacon above each ship is its deadline: green means relaxed, red means it's about to cost you. Ships left too long give up and leave.
2
Earn per box Your crane unloads onto tractors, tractors deliver to the warehouse, and you're paid for every container — with a fat bonus for finishing before the deadline.
3
Spend to grow Click the crane, warehouse, Trade Office, signposts and crew cabin to buy upgrades: faster cranes, more tractors, Berth 2 to work two ships at once, richer trade routes, more workers. Mind the payroll — the crew is paid every dawn whether ships came or not.
4
Expand and export After Berth 2, reclaim land for the Storage Yard, then build the Factory — every dispatched box becomes export goods your cranes load back onto ships for KD 400 a unit. Crown it with the Refinery and exports fetch KD 800. Import, process, export, repeat.
5
Set the pace A new ship arrives about every 90 seconds. Press ⚡ Fast for one every 30 seconds, or unlock Harbor Schedule Control in the Shop to set the timetable yourself.
