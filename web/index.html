WELCOME, CAPTAIN

How to run your port
1
Dock the ships Ships arrive and wait offshore — a red ⚓ Ship waiting button appears at the top. Click it (or the ship itself), then press DOCK SHIP. The beacon above each ship is its deadline: green means relaxed, red means it's about to cost you. Ships left too long give up and leave.
2
Earn per box Your crane unloads onto tractors, tractors deliver to the warehouse, and you're paid for every container — with a fat bonus for finishing before the deadline.
3
Spend to grow Click the crane, warehouse, Trade Office, signposts and crew cabin to buy upgrades: faster cranes, more tractors, richer trade routes, more workers — every menu shows what you can afford in green and what you can't yet in red. Delivery vans run your boxes to the City Depot for cash — buy more vans there. Mind the payroll — the crew is paid every dawn whether ships came or not — and keep the Substation stocked with electricity and oil: an empty port runs at half speed.
4
Expand and export Your port opens with two berths and two cranes — work two ships at once from day one. Click any ship to browse the fleet catalog and buy ships of your own (a PORT CLUB perk). Reclaim land for the Storage Yard, then build the Factory — every delivered box becomes export goods your cranes load back onto ships for KD 400 a unit. The Power Plant ends your electricity bills; the Refinery doubles exports to KD 800 and fuels the port for free. Import, process, export, repeat.
5
Set the pace A new ship arrives about every 90 seconds. Press ⚡ Fast for one every 30 seconds, or unlock Harbor Schedule Control in the Shop to set the timetable yourself.
